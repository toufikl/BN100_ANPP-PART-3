\subsection{General Information}

\subsubsection{Medical Purpose}

The BN-100 LUX is intended to provide controlled thermal
support to newborns and neonates requiring a suitable
warming environment.

Its intended clinical use includes neonatal and pediatric
care areas and delivery rooms, according to applicable
clinical procedures and institutional practices.

\subsubsection{Intended Users}

The device is intended to be operated by appropriately
trained healthcare professionals working in relevant
neonatal, pediatric and delivery-care environments.

The operator is responsible for selecting the appropriate
operating mode and monitoring the infant during use.

\subsubsection{Target Patient Population}

The intended patient population consists of newborns and
neonates requiring thermal support.

\subsubsection{Operating Principle}

The BN-100 LUX provides thermal energy through infrared
radiant heating. The heating source generates infrared
radiation directed toward the infant through a reflector
assembly.

The thermal support provided by the device is controlled
through the device control system and may be monitored
using the skin temperature sensor.

\subsubsection{Device Configuration}

The principal configuration identified in the manufacturer
documentation includes the radiant heating system, control
system, infant bed, structural support system, and associated
mechanical and electrical components.

Depending on the supplied configuration, additional
accessories or functional modules may be provided, including:

\begin{itemize}
    \item skin temperature sensor;
    \item LED phototherapy unit;
    \item spotlight;
    \item X-ray cassette tray;
    \item intravenous support pole or hook;
    \item oxygen cylinder bracket;
    \item low-pressure suction equipment; and
    \item other accessories identified by the manufacturer.
\end{itemize}
