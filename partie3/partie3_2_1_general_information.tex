\subsection{General Information}

\subsubsection{Medical Purpose}

The BN-100 LUX is intended to provide controlled thermal
support to newborns and neonates requiring a suitable
warming environment.

Its intended clinical use includes neonatal and pediatric
care areas and delivery rooms, according to applicable
clinical procedures and institutional practices.

\subsubsection{Intended Users}

The device is intended to be operated by appropriately
trained healthcare professionals working in relevant
neonatal, pediatric and delivery-care environments.

The operator is responsible for selecting the appropriate
operating mode and monitoring the infant during use.

\subsubsection{Target Patient Population}

The intended patient population consists of newborns and
neonates requiring thermal support.

\subsubsection{Operating Principle}

The BN-100 LUX provides thermal energy through infrared
radiant heating. The heating source generates infrared
radiation directed toward the infant through a reflector
assembly.

The thermal support provided by the device is controlled
through the device control system and may be monitored
using the skin temperature sensor.

\subsubsection{Device Configuration}

The principal configuration identified in the manufacturer
documentation includes the radiant heating system, control
system, infant bed, structural support system, and associated
mechanical and electrical components.

Depending on the supplied configuration, additional
accessories or functional modules may be provided, including:

\begin{itemize}
    \item skin temperature sensor;
    \item LED phototherapy unit;
    \item spotlight;
    \item X-ray cassette tray;
    \item intravenous support pole or hook;
    \item oxygen cylinder bracket;
    \item low-pressure suction equipment; and
    \item other accessories identified by the manufacturer.
\end{itemize}

The exact configuration supplied for a particular unit shall
be verified against the manufacturer's applicable packing
list and configuration documentation. The inclusion of any
accessory shall not be assumed without supporting evidence.

\subsubsection{Device Classification and Characteristics}

According to the available manufacturer documentation,
the device is identified with the following characteristics:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Device type:} Neonatal radiant warmer;
    \item \textbf{Medical device type:} Active electrical medical device;
    \item \textbf{Classification stated by manufacturer:} Class I;
    \item \textbf{Applied part:} Type BF;
    \item \textbf{Ingress protection:} IPX0;
    \item \textbf{Mode of operation:} Continuous operation.
\end{itemize}

These characteristics describe the device's stated electrical
safety classification and operating characteristics. The
regulatory classification and applicable classification
rule for the Algerian homologation process are addressed
separately in the relevant regulatory sections of the dossier.

\subsubsection{Expected Clinical Environment}

The BN-100 LUX is intended for use in controlled healthcare
environments, particularly neonatal, pediatric and
delivery-care areas.

The manufacturer specifies the following environmental
conditions for operation:

\begin{itemize}
    \item ambient temperature: $+18\,^{\circ}\mathrm{C}$ to
    $+30\,^{\circ}\mathrm{C}$;
    \item relative humidity: 30\% to 75\%;
    \item atmospheric pressure: 700 to 1060 hPa;
    \item air velocity: less than 0.3 m/s.
\end{itemize}

The manufacturer's specified storage and transportation
conditions are addressed separately in the relevant
technical documentation.

\subsubsection{Expected Service Life}

The manufacturer documentation specifies an expected
service life of 8 years.

The actual service life may depend on operating conditions,
maintenance, inspection, component replacement and
compliance with the manufacturer's maintenance requirements.
