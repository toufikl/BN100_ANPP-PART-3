\subsubsection{Development of the Manufacturing Process}

\paragraph{General}

The \textbf{BN-100 LUX} Infant Radiant Warmer is manufactured through
mechanical processing, structural assembly, electrical and electronic
assembly, integration of the radiant heating system, integration of
the temperature-monitoring and control system, and final device
assembly.

The available manufacturer documentation describes the principal
construction and manufacturing elements of the device and places
manufacturing activities within the manufacturer's quality-management
and risk-management framework.

The manufacturing activities correspond to the principal functional
assemblies and safety-related characteristics of the finished device.

\paragraph{Manufacturing Process Development}

The available manufacturer documentation describes the established
manufacturing and assembly activities associated with the principal
assemblies of the BN-100 LUX, including:

\begin{itemize}
    \item processing and assembly of the principal mechanical
    structures;
    \item assembly of the infant crib and associated protective
    components;
    \item integration of the radiant heating assembly;
    \item integration of electrical and electronic components;
    \item installation of the temperature-monitoring system;
    \item integration of alarm and safety-related functions; and
    \item final assembly of the finished device.
\end{itemize}

A separate, detailed historical record of the successive development
stages of the manufacturing process is not included in the
documentation available for this submission.

Accordingly, this section describes the manufacturing process and
its development only to the extent supported by the available
manufacturer documentation.

\paragraph{Relationship Between Product Design and Manufacturing}

The manufacturing activities correspond to the principal functional
assemblies of the BN-100 LUX, including:

\begin{itemize}
    \item the base and mobile support structure;
    \item the infant crib and protective enclosure;
    \item the radiant heating assembly;
    \item the reflector and heating-element assembly;
    \item the operating and control assembly;
    \item the temperature-monitoring system;
    \item the electrical and electronic control system; and
    \item the alarm and safety-related components.
\end{itemize}

These assemblies are integrated during manufacturing and final
assembly to form the finished Infant Radiant Warmer.

The relationship between product design, manufacturing activities,
and the associated safety and performance characteristics is
considered within the manufacturer's quality-management and
risk-management processes.

The identification of critical components and safety-related
functions in the manufacturer's risk-management documentation
provides a documented link between product design and manufacturing
controls.

\paragraph{Manufacturing Changes}

The manufacturer's Risk Management Report identifies product
change control as part of the control of production and
post-production activities.

Manufacturing and product changes are managed through the
manufacturer's established change-control system.

The available documentation does not identify a specific
manufacturing change, engineering change order, or individual
process revision applicable to the device covered by this dossier.

Consequently, no specific manufacturing change or process revision
is described in this section.

\paragraph{Transfer to Routine Production}

The available manufacturer documentation describes established
manufacturing and assembly activities for the BN-100 LUX Infant
Radiant Warmer.

The relationship between product design, manufacturing activities,
quality management, and risk management is addressed within the
manufacturer's documented processes.

Detailed design-transfer records, process-qualification packages,
and production-readiness records are controlled manufacturer
documents. They are not reproduced in this section where they
have not been provided as supporting evidence for this submission.

Where required for the final regulatory assessment, applicable
manufacturer documentation may be provided as additional
supporting evidence.

\paragraph{Risk Management Considerations}

Manufacturing-process-related risks are considered within the
manufacturer's Risk Management Report for the BN-100 LUX Infant
Radiant Warmer.

The report identifies production and post-production activities
as part of the overall risk-management process. Applicable
manufacturing processes, methods, and control measures are to be
followed in accordance with the manufacturer's established
procedures, while product changes are managed through the
manufacturer's change-control process.

The manufacturer's Risk Management Report identifies
\textbf{EN ISO 14971:2019} and \textbf{ISO/TR 24971:2020} as the
risk-management references applicable to the documented
risk-management process.

This framework provides a documented basis for considering
manufacturing-process-related risks throughout the device lifecycle.
