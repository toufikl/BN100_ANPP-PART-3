\subsubsection{Description of the Manufacturing Process and Operational Controls}

\paragraph{General}

The \textbf{BN-100 LUX} Neonatal Radiant Warmer is manufactured and
assembled by Zhengzhou Dison Instrument and Meter Co., Ltd.

The available manufacturer documentation describes the principal
assemblies, materials, critical components and safety-related
characteristics of the finished device.

The documented manufacturing and assembly activities involve the
mechanical structure, radiant heating system, temperature monitoring
and control system, electrical and electronic assemblies, and
associated accessories.

The available documentation does not provide a complete
manufacturing sequence, detailed work instructions, process
parameters, acceptance criteria or complete production records.
Accordingly, this section describes the documented activities
without introducing additional manufacturing operations or
undocumented process requirements.

% ============================================================
% Mechanical Structure and Base Assembly
% ============================================================

\paragraph{Mechanical Structure and Base Assembly}

According to the manufacturer documentation, the base structure
incorporates aluminium alloy extrusions. The documented processing
activities for structural components include processing, welding
and surface spraying.

The lower part of the device may be equipped with four mobile
castors with brakes, depending on the applicable configuration.

The crib incorporates steel components manufactured using forming,
bending and welding operations, followed by surface finishing.
It also incorporates the documented X-ray cassette arrangement and
a mechanism for adjusting the crib inclination. Protective
Plexiglas panels are incorporated into the crib assembly.

These components are integrated to provide the mechanical support
for the infant crib, operating section and associated assemblies.

The specific mechanical inspection procedures and acceptance limits
are not detailed in the available documentation.

% ============================================================
% Radiant Heating Assembly
% ============================================================

\paragraph{Radiant Heating Assembly}

According to the applicable manufacturer documentation, the
radiant heating assembly comprises:

\begin{itemize}
    \item an aluminium alloy structural assembly;
    \item a stainless steel reflector;
    \item a fixed explosion-proof quartz infrared heating tube; and
    \item a protective net in front of the heating element.
\end{itemize}

The device also incorporates an adjustable-angle LED white light.

The radiant heating assembly is integrated with the control system
to provide the intended controlled thermal environment for the
patient. The heating element and its associated components are
identified as safety-relevant components in the manufacturer
documentation and are considered within the manufacturer's quality
and risk-management framework.

% ============================================================
% Operating and Control Assembly
% ============================================================

\paragraph{Operating and Control Assembly}

The operating section incorporates processed aluminium alloy
components and houses the electrical and electronic assemblies
required for operation of the device.

The manufacturer documentation identifies power transformers,
control circuit boards and associated electrical components within
this assembly.

The operating section is fitted with its corresponding cover and
may support additional components, such as the infusion hook and
carrier tray, depending on the applicable configuration.

The assembly integrates the mechanical, electrical and electronic
components into the finished device. The detailed assembly sequence
and individual production instructions are not provided in the
available documentation.

% ============================================================
% Temperature Monitoring and Control
% ============================================================

\paragraph{Temperature Monitoring and Control}

The \textbf{BN-100 LUX} incorporates a skin temperature sensor and
a temperature control system. The documented skin temperature
sensor is identified as model \textbf{T10-AS}.

The sensor monitors the infant's skin temperature and provides
temperature information to the control system.

The manufacturer's risk-management documentation identifies the
following characteristics of the finished device:

\begin{itemize}
    \item control temperature range: \(32\text{--}38\,^\circ\mathrm{C}\);
    \item display temperature range: \(10\text{--}45\,^\circ\mathrm{C}\);
    \item control precision: \(\leq 0.5\,^\circ\mathrm{C}\);
    \item skin temperature sensor precision:
    \(\leq 0.3\,^\circ\mathrm{C}\);
    \item over-temperature protection at approximately
    \(40\,^\circ\mathrm{C}\);
    \item temperature deviation alarm at approximately
    \(\pm 1\,^\circ\mathrm{C}\); and
    \item alarm in the event of a skin temperature sensor
    short circuit or open circuit.
\end{itemize}

These values describe the documented operating and safety
characteristics of the finished device. They are not presented
here as individual manufacturing-process acceptance criteria.

% ============================================================

\paragraph{Electrical and Electronic Assembly}

The electrical and electronic assembly incorporates components
required for power supply, switching, control, protection,
temperature monitoring, heating and alarm functions.

The manufacturer's risk-management documentation identifies the
following critical components:

\begin{itemize}
    \item dual-fuse input socket;
    \item power supply cord;
    \item auxiliary socket outlets;
    \item relay;
    \item rocker switch;
    \item fuse;
    \item rechargeable battery;
    \item optocoupler;
    \item printed circuit board;
    \item internal wiring;
    \item skin temperature sensor;
    \item air heater;
    \item thyristors;
    \item audible alarm;
    \item switching power supply; and
    \item electrical filter.
\end{itemize}

These components are identified in the manufacturer's
risk-management documentation as relevant to the device's
functionality and safety.

Detailed component-level production specifications, supplier
requirements and individual acceptance criteria are not provided
in the documentation available for this section.

% ============================================================
% Alarm and Safety-Related Operations
% ============================================================

\paragraph{Alarm and Safety-Related Operations}

The finished \textbf{BN-100 LUX} incorporates safety-related alarm
functions documented by the manufacturer, including:

\begin{itemize}
    \item over-temperature alarm and heating cut-off;
    \item temperature deviation alarm;
    \item skin temperature sensor short-circuit or open-circuit alarm;
    \item power failure alarm; and
    \item manual-mode time alarm.
\end{itemize}

These functions are identified as safety-related characteristics
of the device in the manufacturer's risk-management documentation.

The corresponding electrical and electronic components are
integrated during assembly. The specific production test procedures
and acceptance criteria for each alarm function are not detailed
in the documentation available for this section.
