\section{Bibliographical References}

\subsection{General}

The bibliographical and documentary references listed in this section
identify the principal technical and regulatory sources used to prepare
Part III of the technical dossier for the \textbf{BN-100 LUX} Infant
Radiant Warmer.

The references are limited to documents and standards relevant to the
technical information presented in the dossier.

\subsection{Manufacturer Documentation}

\begin{enumerate}

    \item \textbf{BN-100 User Manual}, manufacturer documentation,
    Version 2.2, as available in the technical documentation.

    \item \textbf{Risk Management Report (BN-100/BN-100A)},
    Document No. YF-7.3-24-02 (w), Version A/0.

    \item \textbf{BSI EU Quality Management System Certificate},
    Document No. MDR 796664 R000.

    \item \textbf{ISO 13485 Quality Management System Certificate},
    Document No. MD 796665.

\end{enumerate}

The original title and identification of each manufacturer document
or certificate shall be retained in the submitted technical file.

\subsection{Applicable Standards and Reference Documents}

The following standards are referenced in the available manufacturer
documentation and are relevant to the technical and safety assessment
of the device:

\begin{enumerate}

    \item \textbf{IEC 60601-1} --- Medical electrical equipment ---
    Part 1: General requirements for basic safety and essential
    performance.

    \item \textbf{IEC 60601-1-2} --- Medical electrical equipment ---
    Part 1-2: General requirements for basic safety and essential
    performance --- Collateral Standard: Electromagnetic disturbances
    --- Requirements and tests.

    \item \textbf{IEC 60601-1-8} --- Medical electrical equipment ---
    Part 1-8: General requirements, tests and guidance for alarm
    systems in medical electrical equipment and medical electrical
    systems.

    \item \textbf{ISO 14971} --- Medical devices --- Application of
    risk management to medical devices.

\end{enumerate}

These references identify relevant technical standards but do not,
by themselves, constitute evidence of conformity.

Where conformity to a particular standard is claimed, the corresponding
manufacturer documentation, test report, certificate or other objective
evidence should be provided where applicable.

\subsection{Use of References}

The references listed above support the technical description, safety
assessment, risk management and specifications presented in Part III.

The manufacturer's documentation remains the primary source for
device-specific characteristics and specifications.

Standards are used as technical reference documents and shall not be
interpreted as evidence of compliance unless supported by the
corresponding objective evidence.

\subsection{Document Identification}

For controlled manufacturer documents and certificates, the following
information should be retained whenever available:

\begin{itemize}

    \item Document title;

    \item Document number;

    \item Version or revision;

    \item Date;

    \item Manufacturer or issuing organization.

\end{itemize}

No document identification information shall be created where it is
not provided by the original issuing organization.
