\subsubsection{Manufacturer(s)}

\paragraph{Manufacturer Identification}

The medical device covered by this application is the
\textbf{BN-100 LUX} Infant Radiant Warmer.

The manufacturer identified in the available technical
documentation and quality-management-system documentation is:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Manufacturer:} Zhengzhou Dison Instrument and Meter Co., Ltd.
    \item \textbf{Address:} No. 67, Jinsuo Road, High-Tech Development Zone,
    Zhengzhou, Henan, China
    \item \textbf{Postal Code:} 450001
\end{itemize}

The available manufacturer documentation identifies
Zhengzhou Dison Instrument and Meter Co., Ltd. as the manufacturer
responsible for the device.

\paragraph{Manufacturing Site Information}

The address stated above is the manufacturer address identified
in the available documentation.

No separate manufacturing-site address, additional manufacturing
facility, subcontractor or other manufacturer is identified in
the documentation considered in this section. Accordingly, no
additional manufacturing entity or site is described here.

\paragraph{Quality Management System Certification}

The manufacturer holds an ISO 13485:2016 quality-management-system
certification issued by BSI. The certificate information available
for this submission is summarized below:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Certificate Holder:}
    Zhengzhou Dison Instrument and Meter Co., Ltd.
    \item \textbf{Standard:} ISO 13485:2016
    \item \textbf{Certificate No.:} MD 796665
    \item \textbf{Certification Body:} BSI
    \item \textbf{Original Registration Date:} 05 March 2024
    \item \textbf{Effective Date:} 05 March 2024
    \item \textbf{Expiry Date:} 04 March 2027
\end{itemize}

The certified scope includes the design and development,
manufacture and distribution of the following medical devices:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Baby Incubators;
    \item Infant Radiant Warmers;
    \item Infant Phototherapy Units;
    \item Transport Infant Incubators; and
    \item Reusable Pneumatic Pulmonary Resuscitators.
\end{itemize}

The ISO 13485:2016 certificate is included in the regulatory
dossier as supporting documentation concerning the manufacturer's
quality-management system.

\paragraph{EU MDR Quality Management System Certificate}

The manufacturer documentation also includes the BSI EU Quality
Management System Certificate under Regulation (EU) 2017/745
(MDR), identified as Certificate No. MDR 796664 R000.

The certificate information is summarized as follows:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Certificate Holder:}
    Zhengzhou Dison Instrument and Meter Co., Ltd.
    \item \textbf{Certification Body:} BSI
    \item \textbf{Notified Body Number:} 2797
    \item \textbf{Regulatory Framework:} Regulation (EU) 2017/745
    \item \textbf{Certificate Issue Date:} 22 August 2025
    \item \textbf{Certificate Expiry Date:} 21 August 2030
    \item \textbf{Assessment Framework:} Annex IX of Regulation (EU) 2017/745
\end{itemize}

The certificate scope includes Infant Radiant Warmers classified
as Class IIb within the scope specified in the certificate.

This certificate is included as supporting regulatory
documentation concerning the manufacturer's quality-management
system under the EU MDR framework. It is considered alongside
the ISO 13485:2016 certificate and the other applicable technical
and conformity documentation in this dossier.

\paragraph{Relationship to the BN-100 LUX}

The manufacturer's quality-management-system documentation
covers activities associated with Infant Radiant Warmers.
The BN-100 LUX is identified in this dossier as an Infant
Radiant Warmer.

The ISO 13485:2016 certificate and the EU MDR quality-management
system certificate provide complementary supporting information
concerning the manufacturer's quality-management system and
the activities within their respective certified scopes.

These certificates shall be considered together with the
device's technical documentation, declarations of conformity,
applicable standards and test reports included in the
regulatory dossier.

\paragraph{Documentary Basis}

The manufacturer identification and address presented in this
section are based on the available manufacturer documentation.
The ISO 13485:2016 information is based on BSI Certificate
No. MD 796665. The EU MDR quality-management-system information
is based on BSI Certificate No. MDR 796664 R000.
