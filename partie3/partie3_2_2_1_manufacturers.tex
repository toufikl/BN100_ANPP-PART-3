documentation and quality-management-system documentation is:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Manufacturer:} Zhengzhou Dison Instrument and Meter Co., Ltd.
    \item \textbf{Address:} No. 67, Jinsuo Road, High-Tech Development Zone,
    Zhengzhou, Henan, China
    \item \textbf{Postal Code:} 450001
\end{itemize}

The available manufacturer documentation identifies
Zhengzhou Dison Instrument and Meter Co., Ltd. as the manufacturer
responsible for the device.

\paragraph{Manufacturing Site Information}

The address stated above is the manufacturer address identified
in the available documentation.

No separate manufacturing-site address, additional manufacturing
facility, subcontractor or other manufacturer is identified in
the documentation considered in this section. Accordingly, no
additional manufacturing entity or site is described here.

\paragraph{Quality Management System Certification}

The manufacturer holds a quality-management-system certification
according to \textbf{ISO 13485:2016}.

The certificate information available for this submission is
summarized below:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Certificate Holder:}
    Zhengzhou Dison Instrument and Meter Co., Ltd.
    \item \textbf{Standard:} ISO 13485:2016
    \item \textbf{Certificate No.:} MD 796665
    \item \textbf{Certification Body:} BSI
    \item \textbf{Original Registration Date:} 05 March 2024
    \item \textbf{Effective Date:} 05 March 2024
    \item \textbf{Expiry Date:} 04 March 2027
\end{itemize}

The certified scope includes the design and development,
manufacture and distribution of the following medical devices:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Baby Incubators;
    \item Infant Radiant Warmers;
    \item Infant Phototherapy Units;
    \item Transport Infant Incubators; and
    \item Reusable Pneumatic Pulmonary Resuscitators.
\end{itemize}

The ISO 13485:2016 certificate is included in the regulatory
dossier as supporting documentation concerning the manufacturer's
quality-management system.

\paragraph{Relationship to the BN-100 LUX}

The certified scope of the manufacturer's quality-management
system includes Infant Radiant Warmers. The BN-100 LUX is
identified in this dossier as an Infant Radiant Warmer.

The certification therefore provides supporting evidence
concerning the manufacturer's quality-management system and
the scope of activities covered by the certificate.

The ISO 13485:2016 certificate does not, by itself, establish
the conformity of the individual BN-100 LUX device with all
applicable technical, safety and performance requirements.
Device conformity is addressed through the applicable technical
documentation, declarations, certificates and test reports
included in the regulatory dossier.

\paragraph{Documentary Basis}

The manufacturer identification and address presented in this
section are based on the available manufacturer documentation.
The quality-management-system information is based on the
manufacturer's ISO 13485:2016 certificate, Certificate No. MD 796665,
issued by BSI.
