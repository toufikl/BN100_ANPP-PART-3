\subsection{Description and Composition of the Finished Product}

\subsubsection{General Description}

The finished product covered by this application is the
\textbf{BN-100 LUX} neonatal radiant warmer.

The device is an active electrical medical device intended to provide
controlled radiant thermal support to newborns and neonates requiring
a controlled warming environment.

It provides non-contact radiant heat through an overhead heating
assembly and incorporates a temperature monitoring and control system
using a skin temperature sensor.

The finished device consists of a mechanical support structure,
infant bed, radiant heating assembly, control and operating system,
temperature monitoring system, electrical and electronic components,
alarm functions and associated accessories.

The description presented here is based on the manufacturer
documentation applicable to the BN-100 LUX.

% DEVICE CONFIGURATION

\begin{itemize}

    \item a heating tube;
    \item a control and operating system;
    \item a skin temperature sensing system;
    \item electrical and electronic control components;
    \item audible alarm functions;
    \item an intravenous support pole or hook;
    \item an X-ray cassette tray; and
    \item associated mechanical and electrical components.
\end{itemize}

Depending on the supplied configuration, additional accessories or
functional modules may be provided. The applicable configuration
is determined by the relevant manufacturer configuration and
packing documentation.

% ============================================================
% TEMPERATURE MONITORING AND CONTROL
% ============================================================

\subsubsection{Temperature Monitoring and Control}

The BN-100 LUX incorporates a skin temperature monitoring system.
The sensor provides temperature information to the control system
for thermal monitoring and control.

The manufacturer documentation specifies the following relevant
temperature-control characteristics:

\begin{itemize}
    \item skin temperature control range:
    \(32\text{--}38\,^\circ\mathrm{C}\);

    \item temperature control precision:
    \(\leq 0.5\,^\circ\mathrm{C}\);

    \item skin temperature sensor precision:
    \(\leq 0.3\,^\circ\mathrm{C}\);

    \item overheating level 1:
    \(39\,^\circ\mathrm{C}\);

    \item overheating level 2:
    \(40\,^\circ\mathrm{C}\); and

    \item temperature deviation alarm:
    \(\pm 1\,^\circ\mathrm{C}\).
\end{itemize}

The skin temperature sensor shall be positioned and used in
accordance with the manufacturer's instructions.

% ============================================================
% HEATING SYSTEM
% ============================================================

\subsubsection{Heating System}

Thermal support is provided by the overhead radiant heating system.

The manufacturer documentation identifies an explosion-proof
infrared heating tube rated at \textbf{800 W}.

The heating source is positioned above the infant bed and transfers
heat through non-contact radiant heating. A reflector assembly
directs radiant energy toward the infant bed area.

The heating system incorporates safety functions associated with
temperature monitoring and overheating protection.

% ============================================================
% ALARM AND SAFETY FUNCTIONS
% ============================================================

\subsubsection{Alarm and Safety Functions}

The finished device incorporates alarm and safety functions
associated with temperature monitoring, heating control and
electrical operation.

The manufacturer documentation identifies alarm functions associated
with:

\begin{itemize}
    \item mains power failure;
    \item skin temperature sensor failure;
    \item overheating; and
    \item temperature deviation.
\end{itemize}

These functions are intended to alert the operator to specified
abnormal conditions. Their detailed characteristics and the
corresponding operator responses are described in the manufacturer's
instructions for use and applicable technical documentation.

% ============================================================
% PATIENT CONTACT
% ============================================================

\subsubsection{Patient Contact}

The manufacturer risk-management documentation identifies the infant
mattress and skin temperature sensor probe as components having
patient contact.

The available risk-management documentation identifies patient
contact occurring for more than 24 hours within a 30-day period.

The applicable patient-contact materials and associated biological
safety considerations are addressed in the relevant material and
biological evaluation documentation.

\subsubsection{Non-Sterile and Reusable Device}

as a sterile, single-use medical device.

Cleaning and disinfection shall be performed in accordance with
the manufacturer's instructions for use and applicable institutional
procedures.
