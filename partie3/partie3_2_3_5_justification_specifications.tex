\subsubsection{Justification of Specifications}

\paragraph{General}

The specifications identified for the materials and components used
in the \textbf{BN-100 LUX} Infant Radiant Warmer are considered in
relation to the intended function, safety, and performance
characteristics of the device.

For a neonatal radiant warmer, relevant specifications concern the
mechanical structure, radiant heating system, temperature-monitoring
and control system, electrical power and protection, alarm functions,
and patient-contact materials.

The manufacturer's documentation identifies the principal materials
and critical components considered relevant to device safety and
performance. Their specifications are therefore considered according
to the functions they perform within the finished device.

\paragraph{Patient-Contact Materials}

The manufacturer's Risk Management Report identifies the following
materials as having direct contact with the infant:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Baby mattress:} cotton;
    \item \textbf{Skin temperature sensor probe:} stainless steel.
\end{itemize}

The manufacturer identifies skin contact exceeding 24 hours within
a 30-day period for these materials.

The mattress provides the infant support surface, while the skin
temperature sensor provides the interface required for measuring
the infant's temperature.

These materials are relevant to the intended patient-contact
conditions and are considered within the manufacturer's
risk-management and biological-safety assessment.

\paragraph{Critical Components}

The manufacturer identifies critical electrical and electronic
components used in the \textbf{BN-100 LUX}. Their functions include:

\begin{itemize}
    \item electrical power input and protection;
    \item power supply and switching;
    \item electrical isolation and control;
    \item temperature sensing and monitoring;
    \item radiant heating;
    \item audible alarm functions;
    \item printed circuit boards and internal wiring; and
    \item other safety-related electrical functions.
\end{itemize}

The component models and applicable technical references, where
documented, provide a basis for identifying the components and
relating them to their intended functions within the device.

The manufacturer's Risk Management Report provides the primary
documentary basis for considering these components within the
device risk-management process.

\paragraph{Relationship with Device Safety and Performance}

The specifications of materials and components are relevant to the
safety and performance characteristics of the
\textbf{BN-100 LUX}, particularly the following functions:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Radiant heating:} the heating components are
    relevant to the generation and delivery of thermal energy;
    \item \textbf{Temperature monitoring and control:} the sensing
    components are relevant to monitoring the infant's thermal
    environment;
    \item \textbf{Electrical power and protection:} the power-supply,
    switching, and protection components are relevant to the
    electrical system;
    \item \textbf{Alarm functions:} the alarm-related components are
    relevant to indicating defined abnormal or safety-related
    conditions; and
    \item \textbf{Mechanical structure and patient contact:} the
    structural and patient-contact materials are relevant to
    supporting the infant and providing the intended interface
    between the device and the patient.
\end{itemize}

The manufacturer's risk-management documentation considers hazards
and risk-control measures associated with device construction,
materials, components, and manufacturing activities.

The identified specifications therefore contribute to the documented
technical basis for controlling materials and components relevant
to device safety and performance.

\paragraph{Scope of the Documented Specifications}

The available manufacturer documentation identifies principal
materials and critical components and provides applicable technical
references where documented.

Detailed supplier specifications, complete material certificates,
individual component datasheets, and detailed acceptance criteria
are not reproduced in this section where they are not included in
the supporting documentation available for this submission.

No independent acceptance criteria, analytical limits, or
material-performance requirements are introduced beyond those
documented by the manufacturer.

Where additional detail is required for the regulatory assessment,
the corresponding manufacturer documentation may be provided as
supporting evidence.

\paragraph{Regulatory Conclusion}

The principal materials and critical components identified for the
\textbf{BN-100 LUX} are considered in relation to their intended
functions, patient-contact characteristics, and relevance to device
safety and performance.

The manufacturer's Risk Management Report provides the primary
documentary basis for identifying these materials and components
and considering their associated risks.

Together with the applicable component references, this information
supports the documented technical rationale for the identification
and control of safety- and performance-relevant materials and
components used in the device.
