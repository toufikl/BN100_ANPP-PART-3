\subsubsection{Process Validation and/or Evaluation}

\paragraph{General}

The manufacture of the \textbf{BN-100 LUX} Neonatal Radiant Warmer
involves mechanical fabrication, assembly of the radiant heating
system, integration of electrical and electronic components, and
integration of the temperature monitoring and control system.

The available manufacturer documentation describes principal
manufacturing operations, critical components and
manufacturing-process-related risks.

A separate comprehensive process-validation report or complete
process-qualification dossier specific to the \textbf{BN-100 LUX}
has not been identified among the documentation available for this
submission.

% ============================================================
% Manufacturing Processes Addressed in the Available Documentation
% ============================================================

\paragraph{Manufacturing Processes Addressed in the Available Documentation}

The manufacturer documentation identifies manufacturing and
assembly operations associated with the principal device assemblies,
including:

\begin{itemize}
    \item processing, welding and surface spraying of the aluminium
    alloy base structure;
    \item bending, welding and surface spraying of the steel crib;
    \item fabrication and assembly of the radiant heating structure;
    \item integration of the infrared heating element and reflector;
    \item assembly and integration of electrical and electronic
    components; and
    \item integration of the skin temperature sensor and temperature
    control system.
\end{itemize}

These operations are described at a general technical level.
Separate process-validation protocols, predefined validation
acceptance criteria and documented validation results for each
individual operation have not been identified in the available
documentation.

% ============================================================
% Risk-Based Consideration
% ============================================================

\paragraph{Risk-Based Consideration}

The applicable manufacturer risk-management documentation includes
manufacturing-process risk assessment within the overall
risk-management activities.

It identifies safety-related components and functions and considers
risks associated with the manufacture and operation of the device.

The manufacturer documentation also addresses production and
post-production activities, including applicable product
change-control and customer-feedback-control processes.

These elements provide the available documentary basis for
considering manufacturing-process-related risks. However,
risk-management documentation alone does not establish that each
manufacturing process has undergone formal validation.

% ============================================================
% Process Validation Evidence
% ============================================================

\paragraph{Process Validation Evidence}

The following specific process-validation evidence has not been
identified in the documentation available for this submission:

\begin{itemize}
    \item dedicated process-validation protocols;
    \item documented validation acceptance criteria for individual
    manufacturing processes;
    \item process-qualification reports;
    \item validation records for welding, surface spraying or other
    mechanical fabrication processes;
    \item documented revalidation criteria or revalidation reports;
    \item complete validation records for production equipment or
    manufacturing lines.
\end{itemize}

No validation result or acceptance criterion is stated in this
section unless explicitly supported by the available manufacturer
documentation.

% ============================================================
% Process Evaluation
% ============================================================

\paragraph{Process Evaluation}

The available information permits a documentary-level evaluation
of the manufacturing process through:

\begin{itemize}
    \item identification of the principal manufacturing and
    assembly operations;
    \item identification of critical components and safety-related
    assemblies;
    \item consideration of manufacturing-process-related risks; and
    \item reference to the manufacturer's quality-management and
    risk-management framework.
\end{itemize}

This evaluation describes the information available for regulatory
review and does not replace formal process validation or
qualification where required for a specific manufacturing process.

% ============================================================
% Applicability of Process Validation
% ============================================================

\paragraph{Applicability of Process Validation}

The need for formal process validation depends on the nature of
the manufacturing process and whether its output can be fully
verified by subsequent inspection or testing.

The available documentation does not identify a separate list of
processes designated by the manufacturer as requiring special
process validation.

Accordingly, individual manufacturing operations are not classified
in this section as validated or non-validated in the absence of
corresponding manufacturer evidence.

% ============================================================
% Conclusion
% ============================================================

\paragraph{Conclusion}

The available manufacturer documentation identifies principal
manufacturing and assembly operations and addresses
manufacturing-process-related risks within the manufacturer's
risk-management framework.

Nevertheless, a separate comprehensive process-validation dossier
specific to the \textbf{BN-100 LUX} has not been identified among
the documents available for this submission.

No unsupported statement of successful process validation is
therefore made in this section.
