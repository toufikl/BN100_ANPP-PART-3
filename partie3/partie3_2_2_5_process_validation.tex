\subsubsection{Validation and/or Evaluation of the Manufacturing Process}

\paragraph{General}

The manufacture of the \textbf{BN-100 LUX} Infant Radiant Warmer
involves mechanical processing, assembly of structural components,
installation of electrical and electronic components, integration of
the radiant heating system, installation of temperature-monitoring
components, and final assembly of the device.

The available manufacturer documentation identifies manufacturing
and post-production activities within the manufacturer's quality
management and risk management framework.

These activities are considered in relation to the safety and
performance of the finished device.

\paragraph{Manufacturing Process Evaluation}

The manufacturer's Risk Management Report considers risks associated
with the manufacturing process and identifies production and
post-production activities as part of the overall risk management
process.

Manufacturing activities are required to follow the manufacturer's
established processes, methods, and control measures. The manufacturer
documentation also identifies processes associated with product
change control and customer feedback control.

This documentation provides a basis for considering manufacturing-
process-related risks within the manufacturer's quality management
and risk management framework.

\paragraph{Process Validation}

The available documentation describes the principal manufacturing
and assembly activities and their relationship with the
manufacturer's quality management and risk management processes.

Detailed process-validation protocols, validation reports,
predefined acceptance criteria, and individual validation records
for each manufacturing operation have not been identified among the
supporting documents currently available for this submission.

Accordingly, this section does not state specific validation results
or numerical acceptance values that are not documented in the
available manufacturer documentation.

Where a manufacturing process is subject to validation under the
manufacturer's quality management system, the corresponding
validation activities and records remain part of the controlled
manufacturer documentation.

Where required for the final regulatory assessment, applicable
process-validation documentation may be obtained from the
manufacturer and incorporated into the supporting technical
documentation.

\paragraph{Safety-Relevant Manufacturing Activities}

Particular attention is applicable to manufacturing and assembly
activities associated with safety- and performance-relevant functions
of the device, including:

\begin{itemize}
    \item assembly of the radiant heating system;
    \item installation and positioning of the radiant heating element
    and reflector assembly;
    \item installation and connection of the skin temperature sensor;
    \item assembly of electrical and electronic control components;
    \item installation of power supply, protection, and switching
    components;
    \item installation of alarm-related components;
    \item assembly of protective structures and components; and
    \item final integration of the mechanical, electrical, and
    electronic subassemblies.
\end{itemize}

These activities are relevant to the safe and effective operation
of the finished device and are considered within the manufacturer's
production, quality management, and risk management processes.

\paragraph{Relationship with Risk Management}

Manufacturing-process-related risks are considered within the
overall risk-control strategy described in the manufacturer's
Risk Management Report. The report identifies
\textbf{EN ISO 14971:2019} and \textbf{ISO/TR 24971:2020} as the
applicable risk-management references.

The risk-management process provides a documented framework for
the identification, evaluation, and control of risks associated
with the device throughout its lifecycle.
