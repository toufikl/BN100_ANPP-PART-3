\section{Annexes}

\subsection{General}

The annexes constitute the supporting documentary material associated with
the technical information presented in Part III of the technical dossier
for the \textbf{BN-100 LUX} Infant Radiant Warmer.

The annexes are intended to provide documentary evidence supporting the
device description, manufacturing information, specifications, safety
information, risk management and other technical characteristics presented
in the dossier.

Each supporting document shall retain its original title, document number,
version and identification information as provided by the manufacturer or
the relevant issuing organization.

\subsection{Manufacturer Documentation}

The technical documentation may include the following manufacturer
documents, where applicable and available:

\begin{itemize}

    \item BN-100 user manual and applicable technical documentation;

    \item Risk Management Report (BN-100/BN-100A), Document No.
    YF-7.3-24-02 (w), Version A/0;

    \item Manufacturing process documentation;

    \item Raw material and component documentation;

    \item Finished product specifications;

    \item Applicable inspection and quality-control documentation;

    \item Maintenance and servicing documentation.

\end{itemize}

The original identification of each document shall be maintained in the
submitted technical file.

\subsection{Technical and Safety Documentation}

Where available, the following technical and safety documents may be
included as supporting annexes:

\begin{itemize}

    \item electrical safety test reports;

    \item electromagnetic compatibility test reports;

    \item applicable alarm-system test documentation;

    \item risk management documentation;

    \item applicable certificates and declarations;

    \item technical drawings and diagrams;

    \item relevant component specifications.

\end{itemize}

Only documents actually available and applicable to the
\textbf{BN-100 LUX} shall be included as supporting evidence.

\subsection{Device Identification Documentation}

Documents supporting the identification of the device may be included,
where applicable, such as:

\begin{itemize}

    \item device photographs;

    \item identification labels;

    \item model and serial-number information;

    \item nameplate documentation;

    \item packaging and identification information.

\end{itemize}

The identification information provided in the annexes shall be consistent
with the device subject to the present dossier.

\subsection{Risk Management Annexes}

The manufacturer's detailed risk management records may be provided as
supporting annexes to Section 3.4.

These may include, where available:

\begin{itemize}

    \item hazard identification tables;

    \item risk evaluation tables;

    \item risk-control measures;

    \item residual-risk evaluation;

    \item risk-control verification records;

    \item overall residual-risk assessment.

\end{itemize}

The original manufacturer risk-management records shall be retained without
altering their original document identification.

\subsection{Supporting Evidence}

The annexes may also contain additional evidence required to support
specific statements made elsewhere in the technical dossier.

Such evidence may include manufacturer-issued technical documents,
certificates, reports, drawings, photographs or other controlled records.

No document shall be considered supporting evidence for the
\textbf{BN-100 LUX} unless its applicability to the device has been
established.

\subsection{Document Control}

Each annex should be identified, where applicable, by:

\begin{itemize}

    \item Annex number;

    \item Document title;

    \item Document number;

    \item Version or revision;

    \item Date;

    \item Manufacturer or issuing organization.

\end{itemize}

Where a supporting document has no document number or version, its original
identification shall be retained rather than creating an identification
number that does not originate from the issuing organization.

\subsection{Documentary Basis}

The annexes are based on the technical and documentary evidence available
for the \textbf{BN-100 LUX} Infant Radiant Warmer.

The final annex list shall be completed according to the documents actually
included in the submission package.
