\section{Annexes}

\subsection{General}

The annexes constitute the supporting documentary material associated
with the technical information presented in Part III of the technical
dossier for the \textbf{BN-100 LUX} Infant Radiant Warmer.

The annexes provide documentary evidence supporting the device
description, manufacturing information, specifications, safety
information, risk management and other technical characteristics
presented in the dossier.

Each supporting document shall retain its original title, document
number, version and identification information as provided by the
manufacturer or the relevant issuing organization.

\subsection{Manufacturer Documentation}

The manufacturer documentation included in the technical dossier may
comprise the following documents, where available and applicable:

\begin{itemize}

    \item BN-100 User Manual and applicable technical documentation;

    \item Risk Management Report (BN-100/BN-100A), Document No.
    YF-7.3-24-02 (w), Version A/0;

    \item BSI EU Quality Management System Certificate,
    Document No. MDR 796664 R000;

    \item ISO 13485 Quality Management System Certificate,
    Document No. MD 796665;

    \item Manufacturing process documentation;

    \item Raw material and component documentation;

    \item Finished product specifications;

    \item Applicable inspection and quality-control documentation;

    \item Maintenance and servicing documentation.

\end{itemize}

The original identification of each document shall be maintained in
the submitted technical file.

\subsection{Technical and Safety Documentation}

The technical and safety annexes may include the following documents,
where available and applicable:

\begin{itemize}

    \item Electrical safety test reports;

    \item Electromagnetic compatibility test reports;

    \item Applicable alarm-system test documentation;

    \item Risk management documentation;

    \item Applicable certificates and declarations of conformity,
    including the manufacturer's EC Declaration of Conformity;

    \item Technical drawings and diagrams;

    \item Relevant component specifications.

\end{itemize}

Only documents available in the submission package and applicable to
the \textbf{BN-100 LUX} shall be presented as supporting evidence.

\subsection{Device Identification Documentation}

Documents supporting device identification may include, where
available and applicable:

\begin{itemize}

    \item Device photographs;

    \item Identification labels;

    \item Model and serial-number information;

    \item Nameplate documentation;

    \item Packaging and identification information.

\end{itemize}

The identification information provided in the annexes shall be
consistent with the device covered by the present dossier.

\subsection{Risk Management Annexes}

The manufacturer's detailed risk management records may be provided
as supporting annexes to this section.

These records may include, where available:

\begin{itemize}

    \item Hazard identification tables;

    \item Risk evaluation tables;

    \item Risk-control measures;

    \item Residual-risk evaluation;

    \item Risk-control verification records;

    \item Overall residual-risk assessment.

\end{itemize}

The original manufacturer risk-management records shall be retained
without altering their original document identification.

\subsection{Supporting Evidence}

The annexes may contain additional evidence supporting specific
statements made elsewhere in the technical dossier.

Such evidence may include manufacturer-issued technical documents,
certificates, declarations, reports, drawings, photographs or other
controlled records.

The applicability of each document to the device covered by this
dossier shall be assessed before the document is relied upon as
supporting evidence.

\subsection{Document Control}

Each annex should be identified, where applicable, by:

\begin{itemize}

    \item Annex number;

    \item Document title;

    \item Document number;

    \item Version or revision;

    \item Date;

    \item Manufacturer or issuing organization.

\end{itemize}

Where a supporting document has no document number or version, its
original identification shall be retained rather than creating an
identification number that does not originate from the issuing
organization.

\subsection{Documentary Basis}

The annexes are based on the technical and documentary evidence
available for the \textbf{BN-100 LUX} Infant Radiant Warmer.

The final annex list shall correspond to the documents actually
included in the submission package. Each listed document shall retain
its original identification and shall be included only where available
and applicable.
