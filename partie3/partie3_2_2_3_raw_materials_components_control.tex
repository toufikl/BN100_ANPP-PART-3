\subsubsection{Control of Raw Materials and Components}

\paragraph{General}

The \textbf{BN-100 LUX} Infant Radiant Warmer incorporates
mechanical, electrical, electronic and patient-contact components
relevant to the safety and performance of the device.

The manufacturer identifies critical components and relevant
materials within the applicable product risk-management
documentation. Their selection, specification and control form
part of the manufacturer's product design, manufacturing,
quality-management and risk-management processes.

The available manufacturer documentation provides information
concerning selected component models, technical specifications
and applicable standards, certifications or registration references.

% ============================================================
% PATIENT-CONTACT MATERIALS
% ============================================================

\paragraph{Patient-Contact Materials}

The manufacturer's Risk Management Report identifies the following
materials as having direct contact with the infant:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Baby mattress:} cotton material. The manufacturer
    identifies contact with the infant's skin for more than
    24 hours within a 30-day period.

    \item \textbf{Skin temperature sensor probe:} stainless steel.
    The manufacturer identifies contact with the infant's skin
    for more than 24 hours within a 30-day period.
\end{itemize}

These patient-contact components are included in the manufacturer's
risk-management assessment in relation to the intended use of
the device. The applicable material information and patient-contact
characteristics are documented in the manufacturer's
risk-management documentation.

% CONTROL BASIS

\paragraph{Control Basis}

The critical components identified above are addressed within
the manufacturer's product design, manufacturing,
quality-management and risk-management activities.

The manufacturer documentation identifies applicable technical
standards, component specifications and, where applicable,
certification or registration references associated with the
listed components.

Detailed incoming inspection criteria, supplier qualification
requirements, acceptance limits, inspection procedures and
inspection records are controlled manufacturer documents.
These detailed controls are not reproduced here where they are
not included in the supporting documentation available for
this submission.

% ============================================================
% RELATION TO RISK MANAGEMENT
% ============================================================

\paragraph{Relation to Risk Management}

The identification of critical components is integrated into
the manufacturer's risk-management process. Components associated
with heating, temperature measurement, electrical power,
electrical protection, alarm functions and patient contact are
considered in the assessment and control of risks associated
with the device.

The manufacturer's Risk Management Report, Document No.
YF-7.3-24-02 (w), Version A/0, provides the primary documentary
basis for the identification of the critical components and
patient-contact materials described in this section.
