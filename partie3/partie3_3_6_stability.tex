\subsection{Stability}

\subsubsection{General}

For the \textbf{BN-100 LUX} Infant Radiant Warmer, stability is considered
in relation to the ability of the device and its components to maintain
their specified characteristics during storage, transportation and the
intended service period.

As the \textbf{BN-100 LUX} is a medical electrical device and not a
pharmaceutical product, stability is addressed through the manufacturer's
documented storage conditions, service-life information, environmental
limitations and component lifetime information.

\subsubsection{Storage Conditions}

The manufacturer documentation specifies the following storage conditions
for the device:

\begin{itemize}

    \item Storage temperature: $-40$--$+55\,^\circ$C;

    \item Relative humidity: not more than 95\%;

    \item Atmospheric pressure: 500--1060 hPa.

\end{itemize}

These conditions define the environmental limits specified by the
manufacturer for storage of the device when it is not in operation.

\subsubsection{Operating Conditions}

The manufacturer documentation specifies the following environmental
conditions for operation:

\begin{itemize}

    \item Ambient temperature: $18$--$30\,^\circ$C;

    \item Relative humidity: $30$--$75\%$;

    \item Atmospheric pressure: 700--1060 hPa;

    \item Air velocity: less than 0.3 m/s.

\end{itemize}

These operating conditions are relevant to maintaining the documented
performance and safety characteristics of the device during use.

\subsubsection{Service Life}

The manufacturer documentation specifies a theoretical service life of
\textbf{8 years} for the device.

This service-life value represents the manufacturer's documented theoretical
lifetime of the equipment under the specified conditions of use.

The actual service life may depend on the operating conditions, maintenance,
replacement of components and continued compliance with the applicable
safety and performance requirements.

\subsubsection{Heater Tube Lifetime}

The manufacturer documentation specifies a theoretical operating lifetime
of approximately \textbf{25,000 hours} for the radiant heater tube.

The heater tube is a critical functional component of the radiant heating
system and its documented lifetime is therefore relevant to the expected
service period of the device.

Replacement of components, where applicable, shall be performed in
accordance with the manufacturer's maintenance and service documentation.

\subsubsection{Durability Considerations}

The stability and durability of the \textbf{BN-100 LUX} are related to the
continued integrity and performance of its electrical, mechanical, thermal
and temperature-monitoring components.

The manufacturer documentation identifies critical components and safety
functions that contribute to the safe operation of the finished device.

No additional durability period, accelerated ageing result or stability
study is introduced in this section unless supported by the available
manufacturer documentation.

\subsubsection{Transportation and Storage}

The documented storage environmental limits provide the available
manufacturer basis for assessing the environmental conditions applicable
when the device is not in operation.

The available documentation does not provide a separate accelerated ageing
study or transportation stability study for the finished device.

Accordingly, no additional transportation or ageing performance claim is
introduced in this section.

\subsubsection{Assessment of Stability}

Based on the available manufacturer documentation, the stability-related
characteristics of the \textbf{BN-100 LUX} are documented primarily through:

\begin{itemize}

    \item specified storage conditions;

    \item specified operating conditions;

    \item theoretical service life of the device;

    \item theoretical lifetime of the radiant heater tube;

    \item maintenance and replacement of applicable components.

\end{itemize}

These documented characteristics provide the available basis for addressing
the stability and durability of the finished medical device within the
technical documentation.

\subsubsection{Documentation Limitation}

The available manufacturer documentation does not provide a separate
pharmaceutical-style stability study for the \textbf{BN-100 LUX}.

No such study is considered necessary as a pharmaceutical stability study
because the product is a medical electrical device.

Any additional evidence concerning ageing, long-term durability,
transportation validation or component replacement intervals should be
supported by the corresponding manufacturer documentation where applicable.
