\subsubsection{Designation}

\paragraph{Commercial and Technical Identification}

The medical device covered by this dossier is the
\textbf{BN-100 LUX neonatal radiant warmer}.

The manufacturer identified in the available documentation is:

\begin{quote}
\textbf{Zhengzhou Dison Instrument and Meter Co., Ltd.}\\
No. 67, Jinsuo Road, High-Tech Zone Development,\\
Zhengzhou, Henan, China, 450001.
\end{quote}

\paragraph{Device Designation}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Device name:} Neonatal Radiant Warmer
    \item \textbf{Model:} BN-100 LUX
    \item \textbf{Product designation:} BN-100 LUX
    \item \textbf{Device type:} Active electrical medical device
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Nature of the Device}

The BN-100 LUX is a reusable medical device. It is not a
medicinal product and does not contain an active pharmaceutical
substance.

\paragraph{Identification and Traceability}

For regulatory identification and traceability purposes,
applicable identification information shall be taken from the
device label and the manufacturer's relevant documentation.
This information may include the model designation, serial
number, lot or production identification, and other applicable
identifiers.

The applicable label artwork and identification format shall
be verified against the manufacturer's labeling documentation.
