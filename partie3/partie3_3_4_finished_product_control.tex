\subsection{Control of the Finished Product}

\subsubsection{General}

The \textbf{BN-100 LUX} Infant Radiant Warmer is a finished
medical device intended to provide a controlled thermal
environment for newborn and infant patients requiring thermal
support.

The manufacturer documentation identifies safety-related
characteristics, critical components and functions relevant to
the finished device. This section presents these characteristics
in relation to the documented risk-control measures and the
available finished-product documentation.

% ============================================================
% Safety-Related Finished Product Functions
% ============================================================

\subsubsection{Safety-Related Finished Product Functions}

The manufacturer's Risk Management Report identifies the
following safety-related functions and alarm conditions:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Over-temperature protection with heating cut-off and
    audible and visual alarm;
    \item Temperature deviation alarm;
    \item Skin temperature sensor short-circuit alarm;
    \item Skin temperature sensor open-circuit alarm;
    \item Power failure alarm;
    \item Manual-mode alarm.
\end{itemize}

These functions form part of the documented safety and
risk-control framework for the finished device.

% ============================================================
% Critical Components
% ============================================================

\subsubsection{Critical Components}

The manufacturer's Risk Management Report identifies critical
components associated with the electrical, electronic, heating,
temperature-monitoring and alarm functions of the device,
including:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Dual-fuse input socket;
    \item Power supply cord;
    \item Auxiliary socket outlets;
    \item Relay;
    \item Rocker switch;
    \item Fuse;
    \item Rechargeable battery;
    \item Optocoupler;
    \item Printed circuit board;
    \item Internal wiring;
    \item Skin temperature sensor;
    \item Air heater;
    \item Thyristors;
    \item Audible alarm;
    \item Switching power supply;
    \item Electrical filter.
\end{itemize}

These components are relevant to the documented safety-related
functions and characteristics of the finished device.

% ============================================================
% Patient-Contact Materials
% ============================================================

\subsubsection{Patient-Contact Materials}

The manufacturer identifies the following components as
involving direct patient contact:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Cotton baby mattress;
    \item Stainless-steel skin temperature sensor probe.
\end{itemize}

The manufacturer's risk-management documentation considers
these patient-contact components in relation to the associated
biological safety considerations.

% ============================================================
% Finished Product Safety Considerations
% ============================================================

\subsubsection{Finished Product Safety Considerations}

The finished device incorporates mechanical, thermal, electrical,
electronic and temperature-monitoring functions.

The manufacturer's risk-management documentation addresses
risks associated with these functions and identifies corresponding
risk-control measures.

The characteristics and functions described in this section
complement the specifications presented in Section 3.3.4.1
and their justification in Section 3.3.4.5.

% ============================================================
% Documentation Limitation
% ============================================================

\subsubsection{Documentation Limitation}

The documentation available for this submission does not include
a complete, separate finished-product inspection protocol
specifying individual acceptance criteria and inspection records
for every manufactured unit.

Accordingly, this section does not claim the existence of
unit-specific inspection results, batch-release records or
acceptance criteria that are not described in the available
documentation.

The information presented is limited to the finished-product
characteristics, critical components and functions documented
by the manufacturer.
