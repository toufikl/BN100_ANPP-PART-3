\subsubsection{Composition}

\paragraph{General}

The finished \textbf{BN-100 LUX} Neonatal Radiant Warmer is an
active electrical medical device composed of mechanical, electrical
and electronic assemblies integrated into a single system to provide
controlled radiant thermal support to newborns and neonates.

Its principal assemblies include the structural components, radiant
heating system, temperature monitoring and control components,
electrical power and protection components, alarm components and
associated accessories.

The composition described below is based on the applicable
manufacturer documentation, including the manufacturer's
risk-management documentation.

% ============================================================
% Mechanical Assemblies
% ============================================================

\paragraph{Mechanical Assemblies}

The principal mechanical assemblies of the finished device include:

\begin{itemize}
    \item the base and mobile support structure;
    \item the infant crib;
    \item the protective side panels;
    \item the radiant heating support structure;
    \item the reflector support;
    \item the operating assembly;
    \item accessory support components; and
    \item the X-ray cassette support and tray arrangement.
\end{itemize}

According to the manufacturer's risk-management documentation, the
base incorporates aluminium alloy extrusions and processed, welded
and surface-finished structural components.

The crib incorporates formed and welded steel components with
protective surface finishing. It also includes an X-ray cassette
window or tray arrangement and a mechanism for adjusting the crib
inclination.

% ============================================================
% Radiant Heating Assembly
% ============================================================

\paragraph{Radiant Heating Assembly}

The radiant heating assembly is a principal functional assembly of
the finished device. According to the applicable manufacturer
documentation, it includes:

\begin{itemize}
    \item an aluminium alloy support structure;
    \item a stainless steel reflector;
    \item a fixed explosion-proof quartz infrared heating tube;
    \item a protective burn net; and
    \item an adjustable-angle LED white light.
\end{itemize}

The assembly is positioned above the infant crib and provides the
radiant thermal energy required for the intended thermal-support
function.

Detailed operating characteristics of the heating system are
addressed in the relevant technical sections of the dossier.

% ============================================================
% Control and Operating Assembly
% ============================================================

\paragraph{Control and Operating Assembly}

The operating assembly contains components required for operation,
monitoring and control of the device.

The manufacturer's documentation identifies power transformers and
control circuit boards installed within this assembly.

The assembly also provides support for certain accessories,
including the infusion hook and carrier tray, depending on the
applicable configuration.

% ============================================================
% Temperature Monitoring System
% ============================================================

\paragraph{Temperature Monitoring System}

The finished device incorporates a skin temperature monitoring
system. The principal patient-contact component of this system is
the skin temperature sensor probe, identified in the applicable
manufacturer documentation as model \textbf{T10-AS}.

The sensor provides temperature information to the control system
for monitoring and regulation of the infant's thermal environment.

The manufacturer's risk-management documentation identifies the
skin temperature sensor as a critical component of the device.

% ============================================================
% Electrical and Electronic Composition
% ============================================================

\paragraph{Electrical and Electronic Composition}

The electrical and electronic composition of the finished device
includes components associated with power input, electrical
protection, switching, control, temperature sensing, heating and
alarm functions.

The manufacturer's risk-management documentation identifies the
following critical components:

\begin{itemize}
    \item dual-fuse input socket;
    \item power supply cord;
    \item auxiliary socket outlets;
    \item relay;
    \item rocker switch;
    \item fuse;
    \item rechargeable battery;
    \item optocoupler;
    \item printed circuit board;
    \item internal wiring;
    \item skin temperature sensor;
    \item air heater;
    \item thyristors;
    \item audible alarm; and
    \item switching power supply.
\end{itemize}

These components are identified as relevant to the device's
functionality and safety within the manufacturer's risk-management
process. Their detailed specifications and applicable references
are addressed in the relevant component and technical sections of
the dossier.

% ============================================================
% Patient-Contact Components
% ============================================================

\paragraph{Patient-Contact Components}

The manufacturer's risk-management documentation identifies the
following principal patient-contact components and materials:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Baby mattress:} cotton;
    \item \textbf{Skin temperature sensor probe:} stainless steel.
\end{itemize}

The manufacturer identifies direct patient contact exceeding
24 hours within a 30-day period for these components.

These components and their identified materials are included in the
manufacturer's safety and risk-management assessment.

% ============================================================
% Accessories and Configurable Components
% ============================================================

\paragraph{Accessories and Configurable Components}

Depending on the applicable configuration, the finished
\textbf{BN-100 LUX} may be supplied with manufacturer-identified
accessories and optional components, including:

\begin{itemize}
    \item skin temperature sensor;
    \item X-ray cassette tray;
    \item infusion hook or support;
    \item spotlight;
    \item oxygen support components;
    \item phototherapy equipment; and
    \item suction equipment.
\end{itemize}

Optional components form part of the submitted finished-device
configuration only when specified for that configuration.
The applicable configuration and supplied accessories are to be
identified from the manufacturer's relevant configuration and
packing documentation.

% ============================================================
% Composition and Risk Management
% ============================================================

\paragraph{Composition and Risk Management}

The identification of critical components, patient-contact
components and safety-related assemblies forms part of the
manufacturer's risk-management process for the intended use of the
\textbf{BN-100 LUX}.

The composition presented in this section is based on the
applicable manufacturer documentation. Detailed component
specifications and risk assessments are addressed in their
respective sections of the dossier.
