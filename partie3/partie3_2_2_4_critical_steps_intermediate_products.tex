\subsubsection{Control of Critical Steps and Intermediate Products}

\paragraph{General}

The manufacture and assembly of the \textbf{BN-100 LUX} Infant
Radiant Warmer involve mechanical, electrical, electronic and
functional assembly activities relevant to the safety and
performance of the finished device.

The manufacturer considers risks associated with the device
throughout its design, manufacturing and post-production lifecycle.
Critical assemblies, safety-related functions and manufacturing-
process risks are addressed within the manufacturer's
quality-management and risk-management framework.

The applicable manufacturer documentation provides the basis for
identifying the critical assemblies and functions described below.

\paragraph{Mechanical Assembly Controls}

The principal mechanical assemblies include the base structure,
infant crib, radiant heating support structure and operating assembly.

The available manufacturer documentation identifies processing,
welding, surface finishing and assembly activities associated with
the relevant structural components.

Particular attention is relevant to the integration of structural
components, protective components and movable elements that
contribute to the safe operation of the device.

The infant crib assembly includes the infant support structure,
X-ray cassette arrangement, transparent protective side panels
and the mechanism for adjusting the inclination of the infant
support surface.

The radiant heating support assembly includes the structural
support, reflector arrangement, heating element and associated
protective components.

\paragraph{Radiant Heating Assembly}

The radiant heating assembly is a safety- and performance-relevant
subassembly of the BN-100 LUX.

The applicable manufacturer documentation identifies the radiant
heating element, reflector arrangement and associated protective
components. The manufacturer risk-management documentation
identifies the air heater as a critical component.

Correct positioning, secure integration and appropriate protection
of the heating assembly are relevant to the intended delivery of
radiant thermal energy and to the control of risks associated with
the heating function.

\paragraph{Temperature Monitoring and Control}

The temperature monitoring system incorporates the skin temperature
sensor and associated electrical and electronic control components.
The manufacturer risk-management documentation identifies the
skin temperature sensor as a critical component.

Correct installation and connection of the sensor are relevant
to the temperature monitoring and control functions. The applicable
manufacturer documentation specifies characteristics associated
with temperature control, sensor accuracy and alarm functions.

These characteristics are considered within the safety and
performance requirements of the finished device.

\paragraph{Electrical and Electronic Assembly}

The electrical and electronic assembly includes components
associated with power supply, electrical protection, control,
temperature monitoring, heating and alarm functions.

The manufacturer risk-management documentation identifies the
critical components described in Section 3.2.2.3 of this dossier.
Their correct integration into the finished device is addressed
within the manufacturer's production and quality-management
framework.

\paragraph{Alarm and Safety Functions}

The BN-100 LUX incorporates alarm functions associated with
specified abnormal conditions, including:

\begin{itemize}
    \item temperature deviation;
    \item skin temperature sensor fault;
    \item over-temperature; and
    \item mains power failure.
\end{itemize}

The corresponding sensing, control and alarm components form
part of the safety-related functional assemblies of the device.
Their correct integration is relevant to the safety and intended
operation of the finished device.

\paragraph{Intermediate Products and Subassemblies}

The principal intermediate assemblies identified from the available
manufacturer documentation include, as applicable:

\begin{itemize}
    \item mechanical base assembly;
    \item infant crib assembly;
    \item radiant heating assembly;
    \item operating and control assembly;
    \item electrical and electronic assemblies; and
    \item associated accessory assemblies.
\end{itemize}

These assemblies are progressively integrated during manufacture
and final assembly of the device. The relevant manufacturing and
assembly activities fall within the manufacturer's production
and quality-management processes.

\paragraph{Production Controls}

The manufacturer's Risk Management Report identifies production
and post-production activities as part of the overall
risk-management process. These activities are to follow the
manufacturer's applicable processes, methods and control measures.

The manufacturer documentation also identifies processes
associated with product change control and customer feedback
control. These processes form part of the documented framework
for addressing manufacturing and post-production risks.

Detailed production inspection plans, work instructions, acceptance
criteria, inspection frequencies, intermediate-product release
records and manufacturing-stage verification records are not
reproduced where they are not included in the supporting
documentation available for this submission.

\paragraph{Documentary Basis}

This section is based primarily on the manufacturer's technical
and risk-management documentation applicable to the
\textbf{BN-100 LUX}.

The manufacturer's Risk Management Report, Document No.
YF-7.3-24-02 (w), Version A/0, provides the principal documentary
basis for identifying critical components, safety-related
functions and manufacturing-process-related risks.

The manufacturer's user manual and other applicable technical
documentation provide additional supporting information where
relevant.
