\subsubsection{Description of the Manufacturing Process and In-Process Controls}

\paragraph{General Description}

The \textbf{BN-100 LUX} Infant Radiant Warmer is manufactured and
assembled by Zhengzhou Dison Instrument and Meter Co., Ltd.

The available manufacturer documentation describes the principal
structural assemblies, radiant heating assembly, operating and
control assembly, temperature monitoring system, and associated
electrical and electronic components.

The manufacturer documentation also indicates that production and
post-production activities are conducted within the manufacturer's
quality-management and risk-management framework.

However, the detailed controlled manufacturing procedures,
individual work instructions, process parameters, inspection
criteria and production records have not been identified in the
documentation currently available for this submission.

Accordingly, this section describes only the manufacturing and
assembly activities supported by the available manufacturer
documentation and does not reconstruct undocumented production
procedures.

\paragraph{Main Structural Base}

According to the available manufacturer documentation, the base
structure incorporates aluminium alloy extrusions.

The documented manufacturing operations associated with the structural
components include processing, welding and surface spraying.

The base may be equipped with four mobile castors with braking
mechanisms, depending on the applicable device configuration.

These structural components provide the supporting framework for the
infant crib, operating section and associated device assemblies.

The available documentation does not specify detailed process
parameters, inspection criteria or acceptance limits for these
manufacturing operations.

\paragraph{Infant Crib Assembly}

The infant crib incorporates steel structural components.

According to the manufacturer documentation, the structural
components undergo forming, bending and welding operations followed
by surface finishing.

The crib incorporates an X-ray cassette arrangement and transparent
protective side panels.

The crib structure also incorporates a mechanism allowing adjustment
of the inclination of the infant support surface.

The available documentation does not provide detailed manufacturing
work instructions, dimensional tolerances or intermediate-product
acceptance criteria for the crib assembly.

\paragraph{Radiant Heating Assembly}

The radiant heating assembly constitutes a safety- and
performance-relevant assembly of the BN-100 LUX.

The applicable manufacturer documentation identifies structural
components associated with the radiant heating assembly and describes
the integration of the radiant heating element and reflector.

The manufacturer risk-management documentation identifies the air
heater as a critical component.

The available documentation describes a fixed infrared heating
element and associated protective components.

The radiant heating assembly also incorporates an adjustable-angle
LED white light for illumination of the infant care area.

The available documentation does not provide a complete detailed
production sequence, process parameters or specific intermediate
acceptance criteria for the radiant heating assembly.

Accordingly, no additional manufacturing procedure or acceptance
criterion is introduced in this dossier.

\paragraph{Operating and Control Assembly}

The operating and control assembly incorporates processed structural
components together with the electrical and electronic components
required for operation of the device.

The manufacturer documentation identifies electrical components
including power transformers and control circuit boards.

The assembly also provides mounting and support for applicable
accessories, such as the infusion hook and carrier tray, depending
on the device configuration.

The available documentation does not provide the complete assembly
work instructions or detailed production-control criteria for this
assembly.



\paragraph{Temperature Monitoring and Alarm-Related Assembly}

The BN-100 LUX incorporates a skin temperature monitoring system
and associated alarm functions.

The skin temperature sensor is connected to the control system and
is used to monitor the infant's skin temperature.

The manufacturer risk-management documentation identifies the skin
temperature sensor as a critical component and identifies safety
functions associated with sensor failure, including short-circuit
and open-circuit alarm conditions.

The assembly of the temperature monitoring and alarm-related
components is therefore considered safety-relevant.

However, the available documentation does not provide separate
production-stage test procedures or acceptance records for these
functions.

No undocumented production test method or acceptance criterion is
therefore introduced in this section.

\paragraph{Assembly and In-Process Controls}

The manufacturing process includes, as documented, the processing
and assembly of mechanical structures, integration of the radiant
heating assembly, installation of electrical and electronic
components, integration of temperature monitoring and alarm-related
components, and assembly of applicable accessories and protective
components.

The manufacturer's risk-management documentation indicates that
manufacturing-process-related risks are considered within the
overall risk-management process.

Production and post-production activities are required to follow the
manufacturer's applicable processes, methods and control measures.

Particular attention is therefore relevant to assemblies and
components associated with:

\begin{itemize}
    \item radiant heating;
    \item skin temperature monitoring;
    \item electrical protection;
    \item alarm functions; and
    \item other safety-related device functions.
\end{itemize}

The available documentation does not provide a complete set of
in-process inspection procedures, acceptance criteria or inspection
records for each manufacturing stage.

\paragraph{Production and Post-Production Control}

The manufacturer's Risk Management Report identifies production and post-production activities as integral elements of the overall risk-management process. These activities follow the applicable processes, methods and measures established by the manufacturer and include product change control and customer feedback control.

The manufacturing and quality-management framework for the BN-100 LUX Infant Radiant Warmer is documented through the applicable manufacturer documentation, including the Risk Management Report, the User Manual and the ISO 13485:2016 quality-management-system certificate, where relevant. These documents provide the documentary basis for considering manufacturing, component-control and post-production risks within the device's overall safety and performance framework. The original manufacturer documentation is retained as supporting evidence in the regulatory dossier.
