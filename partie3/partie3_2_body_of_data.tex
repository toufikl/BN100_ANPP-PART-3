\subsection{Scope of the Data}

This section presents the technical information supporting the
characterization of the \textbf{BN-100 LUX} medical device for the
purpose of the medical device registration and homologation dossier.

The \textbf{BN-100 LUX} is a reusable neonatal radiant warmer
intended to provide controlled thermal support to newborns and
neonates through non-contact radiant heating.

The information presented in Part III addresses the physical,
mechanical, electrical, electronic, material, manufacturing,
performance, safety and risk-management characteristics applicable
to the device.

The information is structured according to the applicable requirements
of the homologation dossier and is adapted to the nature and
technological characteristics of the \textbf{BN-100 LUX} as an active
electrical medical device.

% ============================================================
% 3.2.2 Technical Information Covered
% ============================================================

\subsection{Technical Information Covered}

The technical information presented in the following sections covers,
as applicable:

\begin{itemize}
    \item identification and designation of the device;
    \item general physical and functional characteristics;
    \item materials, components and accessories;
    \item device configuration and principal operating functions;
    \item manufacturing processes and manufacturing controls;
    \item controls applied to materials, components and critical
    manufacturing steps;
    \item specifications and characteristics applicable to the
    finished device;
    \item applicable standards and technical references;
    \item environmental, service-life and maintenance-related
    characteristics;
    \item risk-management and risk-control information available for
    the device; and
    \item supporting technical documentation and references.
\end{itemize}

The level of detail provided for each subject is determined by its
applicability to the \textbf{BN-100 LUX}, the nature of the
information concerned and the supporting manufacturer documentation
available for the device.

% ============================================================
% 3.2.3 Applicability to the BN-100 LUX
% ============================================================

\subsection{Applicability to the BN-100 LUX}

The applicability of each technical information category is assessed
according to the nature, intended purpose, technological
characteristics and clinical use of the \textbf{BN-100 LUX}.

Information categories applicable to medical devices are addressed
according to the corresponding characteristics and functions of the
device.

Requirements or information categories that are not applicable to the
\textbf{BN-100 LUX} are not presented as device characteristics.

Where a requirement is determined to be not applicable, its
non-applicability is identified together with an appropriate
technical justification.

This approach ensures that the dossier reflects the actual
characteristics of the device while avoiding the introduction of
unsupported information.
