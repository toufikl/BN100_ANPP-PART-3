% ============================================================

\subsection*{General Information}

The BN-100 LUX is an active electrical medical device assembled
from mechanical, electrical, electronic and functional components
forming an integrated neonatal radiant warmer system.

The manufacturer documentation identifies the principal components,
materials and accessories described in this section. The information
is limited to the components and material characteristics identified
in the available documentation.

% ============================================================
% Principal Components
% ============================================================

\subsection*{Principal Components}

The principal components and associated accessories identified in
the manufacturer documentation include, as applicable:

\begin{itemize}
    \item radiant heating source;
    \item reflector assembly;
    \item control and monitoring system;
    \item infant bed;
    \item mattress and mattress cover;
    \item skin temperature sensor;
    \item base and supporting structure;
    \item castors;
    \item side trays;
    \item intravenous support pole or hook;
    \item X-ray cassette tray;
    \item protective side baffles;
    \item drawer;
    \item spotlight;
    \item LED phototherapy unit;
    \item oxygen cylinder bracket;
    \item electrical power supply and power cable;
    \item alarm system;
    \item internal battery associated with the alarm function; and
    \item other mechanical and electrical components required for
    operation of the device.
\end{itemize}

The accessories and configuration supplied with an individual unit
depend on the applicable manufacturer configuration and packing
documentation.

% ============================================================
% Functional Component Classification
% ============================================================

\subsection*{Functional Component Classification}

For technical assessment purposes, the identified components are
grouped according to their principal functions.

\paragraph{Heating system.}

The heating system includes the radiant heating source and reflector
assembly. The manufacturer documentation specifies a radiant heating
tube rated at \textbf{800 W}. The reflector assembly directs radiant
energy toward the infant bed.

\paragraph{Temperature monitoring system.}

The BN-100 LUX incorporates a skin temperature sensor intended to
measure the infant's skin temperature and provide temperature
information to the control system.

\paragraph{Control and alarm system.}

The control and alarm system incorporates electronic components
responsible for the heating, temperature-monitoring and alarm
functions.

\paragraph{Mechanical structure.}

The mechanical assembly includes the base, infant bed, trays,
supports, castors, protective side baffles and other structural
components required for positioning and operation of the device.

\paragraph{Optional and auxiliary functions.}

Depending on the supplied configuration, additional functions or
accessories may include an LED phototherapy unit, spotlight,
X-ray cassette tray, intravenous support and oxygen-cylinder
support.

% ============================================================
% Material Information
% ============================================================

\subsection*{Material Information}

The manufacturer documentation identifies the following materials
for the specified components or associated equipment:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Carbon steel:} radiant heating tube;
    \item \textbf{Lucite/Plexiglas:} protective side baffles;
    \item \textbf{Polycarbonate:} suction bottle.
\end{itemize}

The available documentation does not provide a complete material
declaration for every component of the BN-100 LUX. The exact polymer
grades, metal alloys, surface treatments, coatings, adhesives and
supplier-level material specifications are not all identified.

No additional material composition or grade is assigned to a
component beyond the information documented by the manufacturer.

% ============================================================
% Patient-Contacting Components
% ============================================================

\subsection*{Patient-Contacting Components}

Components that may contact the patient include the infant mattress
and associated covering surfaces, as well as the skin temperature
sensor when applied to the infant.

The available documentation does not provide a complete material
composition declaration or biological-evaluation dossier for all
potentially patient-contacting materials.

Supporting documentation relevant to these components includes,
as applicable:

\begin{itemize}
    \item material specifications;
    \item identification of the nature and duration of patient contact;
    \item applicable biological evaluation and biocompatibility
    documentation;
    \item cleaning and disinfection compatibility information; and
    \item relevant material safety documentation.
\end{itemize}

% ============================================================
% Sterility and Reusability
% ============================================================

\subsection*{Sterility and Reusability}

The BN-100 LUX is a reusable medical device and is not intended to
be supplied as a sterile, single-use medical device.

Cleaning and disinfection shall be performed in accordance with
the manufacturer's instructions for use and applicable institutional
procedures.

% ============================================================
% Material and Component Documentation
% ============================================================

\subsection*{Material and Component Documentation}

For regulatory assessment, the material and component documentation
should identify the relevant components and their applicable
specifications. Where required, supporting documentation may include:

\begin{itemize}
    \item a complete Bill of Materials;
    \item material declarations for relevant patient-contacting
    components;
    \item applicable biological evaluation and biocompatibility
    documentation;
    \item component and material specifications;
    \item supplier specifications, where applicable; and
    \item relevant certificates or conformity documentation.
\end{itemize}

The absence of a particular material specification in the available
documentation does not, by itself, establish that the material is
unsuitable. It indicates that the corresponding specification has
not been identified in the documentation reviewed for this section.
