\subsubsection{Specifications}

\paragraph{General}

The \textbf{BN-100 LUX} Infant Radiant Warmer incorporates
mechanical, electrical, electronic, and patient-contact materials
and components.

The manufacturer identifies specific materials and critical
components relevant to the safety and performance of the device.
The specifications presented in this section are based on the
manufacturer's technical and risk-management documentation.

No additional material or component specification is introduced
beyond the information documented by the manufacturer.

\paragraph{Patient-Contact Materials}

The manufacturer's Risk Management Report identifies the following
materials as having direct contact with the infant:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Baby mattress:} cotton;
    \item \textbf{Skin temperature sensor probe:} stainless steel.
\end{itemize}

The manufacturer identifies skin contact exceeding 24 hours within
a 30-day period for these materials.

These patient-contact materials are considered within the
manufacturer's risk-management and biological-safety assessment.
Their identification provides the material and patient-contact
information used in the device risk assessment.

\paragraph{Component Identification and Control}

The critical components listed above are addressed within the
manufacturer's product-design, manufacturing, and risk-management
activities.

Where applicable, the manufacturer's documentation identifies
technical standards and associated certification or registration
references for individual components.

The component models, technical references, and associated
documentation provide a basis for component identification and
control within the manufacturer's production system.

Detailed supplier specifications, incoming-inspection requirements,
supplier-qualification criteria, and acceptance criteria are
manufacturer-controlled documentation and are not reproduced in
this section where they have not been provided as supporting
evidence for this submission.

\paragraph{Relationship with Risk Management}

The identification of critical materials and components forms part
of the manufacturer's risk-management process. Particular
consideration is given to components associated with:

\begin{itemize}
    \item patient contact;
    \item radiant heating;
    \item temperature measurement and control;
    \item electrical power and protection;
    \item electronic control;
    \item alarm functions; and
    \item other safety-related functions of the device.
\end{itemize}

The manufacturer's Risk Management Report provides the primary
documentary basis for the identification of the materials and
critical components described in this section.

The component specifications and associated technical references
are considered as part of the documented basis for controlling
components relevant to device safety and performance.
