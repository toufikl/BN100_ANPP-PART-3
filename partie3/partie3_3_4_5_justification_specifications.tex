\subsubsection{Justification of Specifications}

\paragraph{General}

The specifications of the \textbf{BN-100 LUX} Infant Radiant Warmer
are based on the applicable manufacturer documentation and relate
to the intended function, documented performance characteristics
and safety-related functions of the device.

The justification presented in this section explains the
relationship between the specifications in Section 3.3.4.1, the
intended function of the device and the manufacturer's
risk-management documentation.

% ============================================================
% Electrical Specifications
% ============================================================

\paragraph{Electrical Specifications}

The rated supply voltage, frequency, rated power and heater power
define the electrical operating conditions specified by the
manufacturer.

These characteristics describe the device's electrical
configuration and provide the basis for connection to the specified
electrical supply.

The manufacturer's Risk Management Report identifies electrical
components relevant to device safety, including the input socket,
power supply cord, fuse, relay, rocker switch, switching power
supply and filter.

% ============================================================
% Environmental Specifications
% ============================================================

\paragraph{Environmental Specifications}

The specified operating temperature, relative humidity,
atmospheric pressure and air velocity define the environmental
conditions documented for operation of the
\textbf{BN-100 LUX}.

The specified storage temperature, relative humidity and
atmospheric pressure define the documented environmental
conditions applicable during storage.

These limits are included in the specifications to describe the
operating and storage conditions established by the manufacturer.

% ============================================================
% Temperature Control Specifications
% ============================================================

\paragraph{Temperature Control Specifications}

The temperature display range, control temperature range and
temperature control precision are directly related to the
principal function of the \textbf{BN-100 LUX}: providing controlled
radiant heat to newborns and infants requiring a regulated thermal
environment.

The specified bed temperature uniformity relates to heat
distribution over the patient support area.

The skin temperature sensor precision relates to monitoring the
patient's skin temperature and to the operation of the temperature
control system.

These characteristics are therefore relevant to the intended
thermal function and temperature monitoring capabilities of the
device.

% ============================================================
% Alarm and Protection Specifications
% ============================================================

\paragraph{Alarm and Protection Specifications}

The documented alarm and protection characteristics are related
to safety-related functions identified in the manufacturer's
Risk Management Report.

The over-temperature protection, heating cut-off and alarm
functions address excessive temperature conditions.

The documented over-temperature thresholds provide indications
associated with the specified temperature limits. The temperature
deviation alarm indicates deviation from the applicable temperature
condition.

The skin temperature sensor short-circuit and open-circuit alarms
address faults affecting the temperature monitoring system.

The power failure and manual-mode alarms provide additional
safety-related indications identified by the manufacturer.

These characteristics are included in the specifications because
of their relationship to the device's documented safety functions
and risk-control measures.

% ============================================================
% Mechanical Specifications
% ============================================================

\paragraph{Mechanical Specifications}

The documented crib inclination, mattress load and tray load
specifications define the applicable mechanical limits of the
patient support structure and associated components.

The crib inclination specification relates to the positioning
function of the patient support structure. The mattress and tray
load specifications define the maximum loads stated by the
manufacturer for these components.

No additional mechanical limits or acceptance criteria are
introduced in this section.

% ============================================================
% Patient-Contact Materials
% ============================================================

\paragraph{Patient-Contact Materials}

The manufacturer identifies the cotton baby mattress and
stainless-steel skin temperature sensor probe as components
involving direct patient contact.

These components are considered within the manufacturer's
risk-management documentation. Their identification in the
finished-product documentation is therefore linked to the
manufacturer's documented assessment of patient-contact-related
risks.

% ============================================================
% Relationship to Risk Management
% ============================================================

\paragraph{Risk Management Relationship}

The specifications of the finished product are considered
alongside the safety-related components and functions identified
in the manufacturer's Risk Management Report.

The documented electrical, environmental, thermal, mechanical and
alarm characteristics describe relevant aspects of the
\textbf{BN-100 LUX} and their relationship to its intended
function and safety-related performance.

The justification in this section is limited to the relationship
supported by the available manufacturer documentation.

% ============================================================
% Limitation of the Justification
% ============================================================

\paragraph{Limitation of the Justification}

The available manufacturer documentation does not include a
separate document entitled ``Justification of Finished Product
Specifications''.

Accordingly, this section provides a structured explanation of
the relationship between the documented specifications, the
intended function of the device and the available
risk-management information. It does not introduce independent
performance claims or acceptance criteria.
